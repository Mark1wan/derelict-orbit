DERELICT ORBIT - Windows playtest build (flat / desktop)
========================================================

Double-click DerelictOrbit.exe. Nothing to install, no server, no browser.

Windows SmartScreen will likely warn that the publisher is unknown - the build
is unsigned. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

This is the FLAT desktop version. Headset VR is not in this build: the VR path
is WebXR and runs in the Quest browser, not in a native executable.

CONTROLS
  Click the window first - that captures the mouse. Esc releases it again
  (Esc does NOT quit; click back in to keep playing).

  Mouse                look
  Right mouse (hold)   grab a surface within reach. While holding, the mouse is
                       your arm: drag left to pull yourself right. Release to let
                       go with momentum.
  Shift                also grabs, if there is a surface within reach
  W A S D              thrusters, relative to where you look
  Space / C            thrust up / down
  F                    flashlight
  E or left click      use a wall terminal (get close, look at it, hold)
  Ctrl+Shift+N         end the shift early (skip to night)

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
  - Day 1 is deliberately quiet: distant shapes, some bangs, lamps that stutter
    with nothing behind them. If day 1 feels eventful, the intensity curve drifted.
  - Lamps: 3-4 per deck are genuinely broken and stutter all run. Roughly one
    apparition in three also takes the nearest lamp with it. You are not meant to
    tell them apart - but listen for something UNDERNEATH the buzz. That means
    something is there about 7 times in 10.
  - From day 6, look down side corridors at night for a doorway that is warm.
  - Every wall fitting is a handhold - ladders, grab loops, foot restraints,
    valve wheels, locker handles. If you find a stretch of corridor you cannot
    cross hand over hand, that is worth reporting.
  - Frame rate in the power plant and server room (the most fittings).

The deck is generated fresh each run, so two sessions are not the same station.
